# About Grand Central

Grand Central is a personal operations board for someone who works across several organizations at once: a day job, a side business, a nonprofit board, a household. Each comes with its own inbox, its own commitments and its own people.

Most tools pick one of two bad options. They merge everything into one stream, so work bleeds into personal and nothing is findable by context. Or they keep everything apart, so you juggle five tabs and lose track of what's due across them.

Grand Central takes a third path:

- **Accounts are the boundary.** Every email and every todo belongs to exactly one account and is only ever handled through it.
- **Projects and notes cross the boundary without breaking it.** A project points at emails, todos and meetings in any account, but never moves them.
- **Home is the one cross-account view,** and it is read-only.
- **AI helps, it doesn't act on its own.** Claude summarizes, drafts and answers. Anything that reaches the outside world (sending, inviting, deleting) is a human click.
- **It runs on a machine you own.** Your mail stays on your hardware, in a SQLite file you can back up, inspect or delete.

The conventions it is built with, from "every widening of what the system may do is a dated decision" to "delete means Trash, never purge", are in `CLAUDE.md` and `BUILD_SPEC.md` §6.
